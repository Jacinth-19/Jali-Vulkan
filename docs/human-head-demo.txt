JALI — HUMAN HEAD + EXISTING NEURAL CLIPS

CURRENT UPDATE
This guide describes the original existing-clips WebGL checkpoint. A separate
decoded rich-v1 human Vulkan path and new local CPU speech studio now exist;
see docs/rich-vulkan-and-streaming.md and docs/windows-portable.md.

WHAT CHANGED
The three existing neural WAVs now drive a more detailed human character.
This is not the old eSpeak baseline. Rhubarb/PocketSphinx analyzes each actual
WAV with its requested transcript and estimates A-H/X mouth-class intervals.
The viewer maps those classes to the character's facial morph controls.

WINDOWS QUICK START
1. Extract the ZIP completely. Do not run the page from inside the ZIP viewer.
2. Open index.html in a modern Edge/Chrome/Firefox browser with WebGL 2.
3. Click one of the three recordings. The character follows its audio clock.
4. Pause, seek, or click the colored cue strip. The face follows that position.
5. Toggle Follow the recorded voice to compare lipsync on versus off.
6. If interactive rendering is slow, open rendered-lip-closures.mp4 directly.
   That portable video is rendered at 15 fps, independently of live viewer FPS.

THE FREE CHARACTER
Source: met4citizen/TalkingHead, avatars/mpfb.glb
Pinned source commit: 2afe6d79779313db8909badebf46f9805a6c7b70
https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead/blob/2afe6d79779313db8909badebf46f9805a6c7b70/avatars/mpfb.glb

Upstream explicitly describes mpfb.glb as an MPFB/MakeHuman-created avatar
licensed CC0. That statement is retained in licenses/model-source-README.md,
with the CC0 text. We do not assume the software's license covers the model.
The original is a full character, shown here in a head-and-shoulders view:
53,116 character vertices, a 67-bone skeleton, and 66 facial controls on the
main mesh. Teeth, tongue, brows, eyes and lashes have additional/matching
controls. This is a generated character, not a claimed real speaker identity.

We resized textures to <=1024 and quantized/Meshopt-compressed the GLB for the
browser. All source bones and facial targets remain in the downloadable file.
Only 20 facial controls actually used by this demo are uploaded at runtime to
reduce shader work; unused source controls were not silently removed from the
asset. Meshopt support is required to open the optimized GLB. For DCC tools
without that extension, download the uncompressed original at the source URL.

TIMING AND QUALITY — WHAT IS AND ISN'T PROVEN
- The WAVs are the exact previously generated Arena stock-voice recordings.
  They are not synthesized locally, not new cloning, and not a user's voice.
- Cue times come from their real audio, not guessed word lengths, a reused
  eSpeak timeline, or an RMS jaw-only animation.
- Rhubarb 1.13.0 PocketSphinx produces automatic, dialog-guided estimated mouth
  classes. This is NOT manually verified phoneme-level forced alignment.
- Nine classes are mapped approximately to the available 3D visemes, with
  35 ms transition anticipation. Retargeting/artistic calibration can improve.
- Blinking/head movement are procedural. Expression styling is manual, not an
  emotion model. Toggle them independently.
- HTML audio currentTime drives the displayed pose. Playback-output/device and
  display latency have not been physically measured; the timing trim is manual.
- This demo renders with Three.js/WebGL, NOT the native Vulkan importer.
  That strict native importer does not support this richer 67-bone/PBR asset yet.
- The 15 fps example video uses the lip-closure WAV and the same calculated
  cues, rendered offline in the browser and muxed into H.264/AAC. No eSpeak
  audio or canned source face animation is substituted.
- Your Windows/i5 10th-generation U/16 GB laptop has not been benchmarked.
  Hardware acceleration in your browser may help; no frame-rate promise is made.

VALIDATION AND PROVENANCE
See manifest.json, checksums.json, cues/*.json, and proof/.
Each cue file records the actual WAV hash, dialog hash, analyzer hash and method.
The original and optimized model hashes, source URL/commit, license statement,
and precise transformations are retained. Original WAV hashes are unchanged.
The official glTF Validator reports zero errors and nine warnings on decoded
geometry: eight non-root skinned-mesh warnings and one clothing tangent-space
warning. They are retained, not hidden or called a zero-warning result.
The compressed-container report additionally notes that the validator itself
cannot decode Meshopt. We separately decode and validate the actual data.
Browser checks cover HTTP and offline file loading, actual morph changes,
play/pause/seek, all three WAVs, sync disable, natural rest, and mobile layout.

BUILDING AGAIN (DEVELOPERS)
Linux/Python 3.11 and the repository Node dependencies are used to build, not
required by the delivered browser pack. The Rhubarb executable/acoustic data
are pinned and cached privately under .cache, not redistributed in this ZIP.
The used binary is from a checksum-pinned npm repack of Rhubarb 1.13.0; this
is provenance, not a claim of an independent upstream binary audit.
No models, training data, voice recordings or secrets are uploaded to Actions.
Dependencies' notices are under licenses/. No project-wide distribution license
has been selected; do not interpret this pack as a grant of unrelated model rights.
