Eyes, idle & manual expression
NOT INFERRED EMOTION









Natural mode uses deterministic irregular blinks, small layered head motion and eased gaze changes. Disable “Procedural blinking & head motion” above to freeze those layers. Idle animates without speech; pause freezes a selected recording. No webcam or emotion analysis.


Timed manual expressions


Start/end are seconds on the completed recording (absolute paragraph time during batching). Up to 32 events, 0–60 seconds. Fade must be no longer than half the event. Overlaps blend; speech closures/contact/rounding protect the mouth. Invalid edits are not applied.

SAME VERIFIED WAV






Choose a completed verified recording, not an in-flight chunk. JSON/CSV use fixed media-time samples with named weights and head/eye radians. WebM renders every frame offline with a fixed media clock, then encodes the same hash-verified WAV as Opus audio. Slow rendering takes longer rather than dropping frames. Requires WebCodecs in current Chrome over HTTPS/localhost. Cancel discards incomplete output. This is WebM, not MP4 or universal FBX retargeting.
